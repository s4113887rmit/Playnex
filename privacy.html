Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, including your username, email address, and profile details. We also collect data about your purchases, wishlist items, and community activity on Playnex.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and improve our services, process transactions, communicate with you about your account, and personalise your experience on Playnex.

3. Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the platform, subject to confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your account data at any time through your profile settings. You may also request a copy of your data by contacting our support team.

6. Cookies

Playnex uses essential cookies for functionality such as maintaining your login session. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. You will be notified of significant changes via the email address associated with your account.
